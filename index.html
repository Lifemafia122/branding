Our Story

OurStory

Origin · Japan
Born in Japan
Croissant Taiyaki was founded by Hotland Co., Ltd. in Japan — evolving from the traditional Usukawa dough into a unique 24-layer authentic croissant recipe unlike anything else.
2013
The 24-Layer Formula
The revolutionary croissant dough was perfected into the signature texture that became the foundation of the brand.
2014 · Thailand
Landing in Thailand
Splendid Co., Ltd. traveled to Japan and secured the Master Franchise license — bringing the original recipe to Thailand as its first and only authorized operator.
Pop-Up Era
First Pop-Up in Thailand
Launched as pop-up stores in Thonglor and Siam Square. Response exceeded all expectations — queues of up to 4 hours proved the concept had arrived.
Events
Major Event Presence
Featured at Thaniya Japan Festival, Dragon Street Market @Emsphere, The Mall, Central Ladprao, and OURS @Charoen Nakhon — building wide brand awareness across Bangkok.
Now · 2026
5 Branches & Growing
5 active branches across Bangkok, with a Franchise Plan targeting 100 locations in 5 years and a daily sales goal of 15,000 per branch.

Growth Strategy

How we growthe brand

Croissant Taiyaki already owns three things nobody can copy: a shape people remember, a 24-layer croissant texture, and a genuine Japanese-original story. Growth is not a campaign with an end date — it is a standing way of working: make people recognise us, make them crave the product, and make coming back the easy choice.

RECOGNISE

Be the brand people picture when they think “fish croissant”.

CRAVE

Show the crisp, the steam, the pull — appetite before argument.

RETURN

Give a reason for the second, fifth and twentieth visit.

Six ways we play

The playbook

01
Own the original story
Japanese original, 24 layers, first in Thailand — say it everywhere, always with real product photography rather than graphics. The story is what makes the price make sense.
02
Lead with hero menu, not everything
Push 3–5 flavours hard so there is always an obvious first order, and rotate a seasonal flavour so regulars have something new to come back for.
03
Let customers do the talking
The product films itself: the tear, the crunch, the filling. Own one hashtag, reward posts with something small, and repost customers every week.
04
Sell where the smell travels
Booths and pop-ups are the strongest format we have — visible baking, aroma, instant purchase. Every event is also a content shoot and a follower drive.
05
Trust beats fame
Steady micro-creators who genuinely live near a branch move more product than one celebrity post. Boost only what already performs on its own.
06
Make returning the easy choice
Stamp cards, bundle sets for offices and catering, off-peak offers, and a LINE OA that tells regulars what is new before anyone else.
